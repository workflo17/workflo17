# Arcane Computing — logo directions

First round of logo concepts for Arcane Computing, Inc. (Scott Livingston), after Scott
wrote on 18 September 2026 asking whether I do logos.

Five **directions**, not five drafts of one idea. They disagree with each other on purpose:
the useful answer back is which one belongs on a van, not which drawing is prettiest.

## What I designed against

Full-service IT for small and mid-size businesses across Chicago's northwest suburbs, in
business since 1996 — networks, servers, PCs and phone systems, for legal, non-profit,
medical and home-office clients. That client list is the whole brief: the mark has to look
like it belongs on an invoice to a law firm *and* survive being cut in vinyl on a service
van.

Everything above came from public listings, not from Scott. If any of it is wrong, the
drawings change.

## The five

| # | Name | The idea | Palette |
|---|------|----------|---------|
| 01 | **Seal** | The A as a rune, inside a seal of office. Leans hardest on the name. | Midnight, brass, parchment |
| 02 | **Keystone** | Flat-topped A — the stone that holds the arch up. The corporate one. | Graphite, teal, mist |
| 03 | **Astrolabe** | A star chart and a network diagram are the same drawing. | Navy, gold, chalk |
| 04 | **Ward** | A vault with a keyhole, and a circuit trace running out of it. | Vault green, copper, bone |
| 05 | **Trace** | One wire, one via. Name set in the typeface of the work. | Ink, vermilion, paper |

## The files

```
svg/0N-name.svg            full colour
svg/0N-name-mono.svg       one colour, solid (dark ink)
svg/0N-name-reversed.svg   one colour, reversed (white)
index.html                 contact sheet — open it in a browser
```

Every mark is drawn on a 120×120 grid as hand-written SVG: no raster anywhere, no
generator output, nothing to redraw when it goes from a van door to a 16-pixel tray icon.
The one-colour versions exist because vinyl cutters, etching and embroidery cannot take a
second colour.

Full presentation boards — each lockup light and dark, the size tests from 56px down to
16px, and the marks applied to business cards, a van door and a browser tab — are in the
design canvas that goes with this folder.

## If one of these is close

Say which number and what is wrong with it. A direction gets refined once it is chosen;
refining all five first is how logo projects die.
